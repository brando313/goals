# Goalie Reads

An iPad training app for young hockey players (ages 6 to 9). It teaches seven goalie reads, one rule each, and lets the player practice finishing plays at the net.

**The three things every rep starts with:** look at the goalie, find the open net, shoot there or go around him.

| What the goalie does | The rule | Best play |
|---|---|---|
| Stays deep in his net | Open net? Shoot now. | Shoot high |
| Drops down early | He drops? Go high. | Shoot high |
| Opens his legs | Legs open? Five-hole. | Five-hole |
| Leans to one side | He goes one way? Shoot the other way. | Shoot far side |
| Comes way out | He comes out? Go around him. | Deke around him, either side |
| Stands tall and waits | He's set? Make him move first. | Fake a shot, then carry across |
| You're on a bad angle | Bad angle? Don't shoot into him. | Carry it across the front, then shoot |

- Shot spots are always shown on the net. Dekes are shown as solid routes on the ice, with a dashed line for the shot that follows.
- Only the best play earns a star. The best play always scores. Other plays sometimes score, and the coach explains the better one.
- Each debrief shows the rule, the best spot or route on the picture, and a view from the stick blade as well as from the eyes.
- Works offline after the first load. Open it in Safari, then tap Share and choose "Add to Home Screen."

Files: `index.html` is the app, `sw.js` keeps it working offline, `three.min.js` draws the 3D view, and `manifest.json` plus the icons set up the Home Screen app.
