# Goalie Reads

An iPad training app that teaches young hockey players to read the goalie and pick the best scoring play: shoot, deke, or go behind the net.

- First-person view of a 3D goalie, from the slot, the wing, sharp angles, and the goal line
- 16 goalie reads with lessons, and debriefs that compare what the player's eyes see with what the stick blade sees
- Left and right shot settings, goalie size by age, learning mode and game speed
- Works offline after the first load. Open it in Safari, then tap Share and choose "Add to Home Screen."

Files: `index.html` is the app, `sw.js` keeps it working offline, `three.min.js` draws the 3D view, and `manifest.json` plus the icons set up the Home Screen app.
